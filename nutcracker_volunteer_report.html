🎯 Strategic Rules of Engagement for Ballet Parents

How to satisfy school volunteer quotas without sacrificing your family's experience:

1. The "Opposite-Cast" Hack

Dancers almost never perform in all 4 shows (Sat 1pm/5pm, Sun 1pm/5pm). If your performer dances in Cast A (e.g. Sat 1pm & Sun 5pm), volunteer during Cast B (Sat 5pm or Sun 1pm)! You watch your child's shows 100% free as a spectator.

2. The "Front-of-House Watch" Roles

Boutique Sales explicitly permits volunteers to sit and watch the performance! Ticket Scanner shifts end right as the curtain rises (1:15pm or 5:15pm). Parent Ushers can stand and watch from the back of the auditorium.

3. The "Zero Show-Day Conflict" Roles

Want zero stress on performance weekend? Take Clean Up Crew (Sunday 7:00pm–8:30pm after all shows wrap) or Wardrobe Prep/Packup. You satisfy all hours outside of performance times completely.
